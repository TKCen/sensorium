## 2026-07-01 - Standardize quote style in invitations and pointers
**Learning:** Standardizing quote style (from curly ‘ ’ or “ ” to straight " ") in CLI invitations and context pointers improves copy-paste usability and ensures consistent LLM command parsing across different environments and terminal emulators.
**Action:** Use standard double quotes (") for commands and key phrases within user-facing strings to ensure high reliability and compatibility with automated parsing and screen readers.
