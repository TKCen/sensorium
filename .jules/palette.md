## 2025-05-15 - [Visual Cues for Context-Injected Salience]
**Learning:** In text-heavy context windows, small visual anchors (emojis) and natural phrasing help distinguish between different levels of autonomous attention (threads vs candidates) and make the system feel less robotic.
**Action:** Use 🧵 for conscious threads and ✨ for raw salience signals in headers and invitations. Prefer standard double quotes for commands/keywords to ensure cross-platform compatibility and visual consistency.
